What I supervise

45 bachelor's and master's projects since 2022. Rather than a list of past titles, these are the topics that work has actually clustered around — and the kind of project I can supervise well.

Technical debt, managed

  • Detecting technical debt with machine learning, including graph-based models
  • Bots and tooling that surface debt where developers already work - issue trackers, pull requests
  • What practitioners actually need from debt-management tools
  • How design degradation relates to the debt a team carries

Architecture, code, and the AI in between

  • Testing and evaluating LLM-based systems, including retrieval-augmented applications
  • Language models as assistants for engineering and for research work itself
  • Tooling for design quality and pattern detection
  • Infrastructure for empirical software engineering: repository mining and replication packages

Infrastructure as software

  • Cost awareness in infrastructure-as-code: finding, predicting and repairing expensive patterns
  • Energy and environmental footprint of running systems, from model training to workflows
  • Cloud deployment and operations in industrial settings
